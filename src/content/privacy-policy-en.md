# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Right Space, operating as El Radd ("El Radd," "we," "us," or "our"), is committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your customers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data when you use our AI receptionist service (the "Service").

This Policy applies to:
- **You, the Client:** The business that signs up for and uses the Service.
- **Your Customers:** The individuals who contact your business through the channels we operate on your behalf (WhatsApp, website chat, and email).

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you must not use the Service.

We comply with the applicable provisions of Egyptian Law No. 151 of 2020 on Personal Data Protection, to the extent required for our operations within the Arab Republic of Egypt.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 From You (the Client)

When you register for the Service, we collect:
- Business name and trade name
- Contact person's full name
- Email address and phone number
- Business address and tax registration number (if applicable)
- WhatsApp Business account information
- Gmail or Microsoft 365 email address (if the email channel is enabled)
- Payment confirmation references (we do not collect or store credit card numbers or banking credentials)
- Support communications: Support tickets, email correspondence, and messages sent through our official support channels

### 2.2 From Your Customers (End Users)

When your customers contact you through the Service, we process the following data on your behalf:
- **Contact:** Name, email address, phone number, company name
- **Communication Content:** Message content (text and voice), media files shared during conversations, email subject and body, attachments
- **Lead Qualification:** Budget, service requirements, timeline, and other qualifying information your customers provide during AI conversations
- **Metadata:** Timestamps, email headers, IP addresses (for security), device type, browser language, time zone
- **WhatsApp-specific:** WhatsApp display name, phone number, delivery receipts

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Data

We collect certain technical data automatically to operate and secure the Service:
- Log data and server timestamps
- IP addresses (for security and abuse prevention)
- Error logs and diagnostic data
- Aggregated usage statistics (for internal analytics and service improvement)

We do **not** use this data to build advertising profiles or track users across third-party websites.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

### 3.1 Client Data

We use your business and contact information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Provide the Service, including setup, training, and ongoing support
- Process payments and maintain billing records
- Communicate with you about your account, service updates, or technical issues
- Comply with legal and tax obligations

### 3.2 Customer Data (Processed on Your Behalf)

We process your customers' personal data solely to:
- Generate AI-powered responses to their inquiries
- Transcribe voice notes to text (using a third-party speech-to-text provider)
- Route complex inquiries to human escalation when necessary
- Maintain conversation history so the AI can provide context-aware responses
- Generate usage reports and analytics available upon request through our support channels

### 3.3 AI Processing

All message content is processed by our AI inference provider to generate responses. Data and conversation patterns specific to your business are used solely to operate and improve your instance of the Service. They are **not** used to train general AI models accessible to other clients, nor are they shared with third parties for AI training purposes.

## 8. Data Retention

### 8.1 Active Accounts

We retain conversation data and account information for as long as your account remains active and you continue to use the Service.

### 8.2 Terminated Accounts

Upon termination of your account:
- We will delete or return your data and your customers' data within **30 days**, per your instructions.
- Where an account is terminated due to non-payment, post-termination data return or deletion assistance is suspended while amounts remain unpaid, to the maximum extent permitted by law. Rights you hold under mandatory applicable Egyptian law are not affected.
- Billing records, transaction history, and tax-related metadata will be retained for **at least 7 years** to comply with Egyptian legal and accounting requirements.

### 8.3 Backup Retention

Deleted data may remain in encrypted backup systems for up to **30 days** after deletion from active databases. These backups are overwritten on a rolling basis and are not accessible for operational use.

### 8.4 Non-Identifying Metadata

We permanently retain anonymized metadata including message counts, timestamps, IP addresses (anonymized), usage statistics, and system logs for billing accuracy, fraud prevention, service optimization, and security analysis. This metadata does not include message content or personal identifiers.

## 9. Data Security

We implement the following technical and organizational security measures:
- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between you, your customers, and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL.
- **Encryption at rest:** Data stored in our databases is encrypted.
- **Access controls:** Staff access is restricted by role and granted on a need-to-know basis. All staff with data access are based in Egypt.
- **Regular monitoring:** We monitor our systems for unauthorized access and security vulnerabilities.
- **Conversation access:** By default, only our technical staff responsible for system maintenance and you, the client, can access your conversation history and customer data. No sales, marketing, or non-technical staff have routine access to your message content.

Despite these measures, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

**Breach Notification:** In the event of a data breach affecting personal information, we will notify affected clients within **72 hours** of our discovery or confirmation of the breach -- not from when it may have occurred -- via email, providing the scope and remediation steps.

Under Egyptian Law No. 151 of 2020 and applicable regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right to rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@elradd.com. We will acknowledge receipt within **72 hours** and provide a substantive response within **10 business days**. Complex requests may require additional time, with notification of extension.

We verify identity using account credentials, email confirmation, or business documentation to prevent unauthorized access. We may deny deletion requests where retention is required by law or for security investigations.

Unsubscribing is automated and takes effect immediately, and is fully applied within 24 hours at most from the time of the request.
